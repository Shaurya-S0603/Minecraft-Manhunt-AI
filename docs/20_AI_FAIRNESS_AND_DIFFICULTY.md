# AI fairness and difficulty tuning

Server access does not justify omniscience. Define what information each difficulty may observe.

## Observation levels

Debug: exact opponent location available. Normal: delayed tracking direction or last-known position. Advanced: line of sight, memory, and in-world signals only.

## Difficulty controls

Tune update rate, planning lookahead, tracking delay, action precision, resource-management competence, and allowed recovery strategies. Do not make the game harder by secretly altering damage or giving unannounced information advantages.

## Evaluation

Use win rates across seed categories, average chase duration, stuck frequency, and match completion rate rather than one anecdotal demonstration.
